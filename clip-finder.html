Prompt 1: Moment finder


You're an editor for a short-form clipping channel. I'm going to paste the transcript of a livestream, with timestamps. Find the 5 best moments to post as vertical clips on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

A good moment works on its own, with no context: a reaction with a clear cause, a punchline, a fail, a clutch play, a story with an ending, or a hot take. Loud isn't the same as good. Skip anything that needs the viewer to know what happened earlier.

For each moment, give me:
1. Start and end time. Start a few seconds before the setup and end just after the payoff. Aim for 15 to 45 seconds; let the story decide the length.
2. What happens, in one line.
3. Why it works as a clip, in one line.
4. The first 3 seconds: what the clip should open on (usually the biggest reaction or the payoff).
5. A hook title: 2 to 6 words, in capitals, that makes people want the ending without giving it away. Never claim something that didn't happen.
6. A spoiler warning if the moment gives away a story or plot point.

Rank them strongest first. Then list up to 3 runners-up with just the times and one line each.

If the transcript is cut off or too long to read fully, tell me which part you covered.

Prompt 2: Hook titles


Write 8 hook titles for a short clip. Rules: 2 to 6 words, all capitals, make people want to see the ending without giving it away, no clickbait that the clip doesn't deliver, no story spoilers.

Mix these styles: a surprise ("HE DIDN'T SEE IT COMING"), chat or the crowd ("CHAT KNEW BEFORE HIM"), a number ("3 SECONDS LEFT"), and irony ("HE SAID IT WAS EASY").

Then tell me which one you'd pick and why, in one line.

Prompt 3: Post text


Write the post text for this clip on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Include:
- A first line that hooks, different from the on-screen title
- The credit line exactly as I give it below
- Any hashtags or tags the clipping campaign requires (I'll list them)
- 3 to 5 relevant hashtags, no more

Keep it under 150 characters before the hashtags. No promises the clip doesn't deliver.
